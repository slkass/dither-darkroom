Darkroom adapts the rounded rectangle SDF, edge refraction, RGB dispersion,
tint and directional glint formulas into a local Canvas CPU rasterizer.
It adds image-relative controls, bilinear sampling, anti-aliasing, and flat-edge
normals. It does not bundle the upstream DOM/WebGL runtime.
